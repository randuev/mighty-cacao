Built on beans with backbone

Mighty Cacao started as a weekend roaster with one stone grinder and a stubborn belief: chocolate should taste like where it came from. We work directly with two cooperatives in Ecuador's Sumaco valley, pay above fair-trade rates, and roast in batches small enough to name.

No vanillin shortcuts, no skim-milk powders, no mystery. Just cacao, cane sugar, and the occasional sea salt — delivered fast so it reaches you the way we packed it.

Frequently asked

Do you deliver outside Sydney?

Yes — regional NSW and interstate is posted via courier, $10 flat, 2–4 business days. Metro same-day is at checkout by default.

What if nobody's home?

Tick “leave at door” at checkout, or your driver will text you to reschedule the window at no charge.

How fresh is “mighty fresh”?

Everything is roasted within 72 hours of delivery. Best before dates are 12+ months out on dry goods.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes — pay by card on pickup at the door, or pay online at checkout. Whichever you prefer.
